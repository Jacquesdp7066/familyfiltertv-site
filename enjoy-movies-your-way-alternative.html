Family Filter TV for Android TV and Google TV: what it covers

Family Filter TV is a narrow tool: an app that mutes spoken profanity on Android TV and Google TV while the picture keeps playing. If swearing is the only thing you want gone and you watch on an Android TV or Google TV, it is built for that. If you want to see what other filtering services cover, check their own sites.

What Family Filter TV does

Family Filter TV mutes profanity in speech while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped. It runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone.

About Family Filter TV
What it filtersSpoken profanity only, muted while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped. It does not filter violence or nudity.
Where it runsAndroid TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi. It does not run on Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV.
What you needAn Android TV or Google TV device and an Android phone. It depends on subtitles being available for the title.
How it worksWorks with Stremio on the TV. In follow-along mode (version 0.2.21 or newer) you watch Netflix or another app on the Android TV, pick the film or episode on the phone, and the TV mutes the swearing. English titles only for now. Filter levels are Off, Mild, Family and Strict, set once on the phone for every linked device.
Price14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan; current prices are shown in Google Play and charged in local currency. Three devices included, up to six.

Is it right for you?

  • It fits if you want swearing muted on an Android TV or Google TV, with Stremio on the TV or by following along while you watch Netflix and other apps.
  • It fits if you prefer one household setting on your phone (Off, Mild, Family or Strict) that applies to every linked device, with three devices included and up to six.
  • If you want filtering beyond spoken language, or you watch on a device this page does not list, it is not the tool for that.

Questions

Does Family Filter TV filter violence or nudity?

No. It filters spoken profanity only, and it depends on subtitles being available for the title.

Is Family Filter TV a streaming service?

No. It is not a streaming service and provides no films or series. It works alongside the apps you already use.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV. You pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

Which TVs does it run on?

Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the Android phone over Wi-Fi. It does not run on Roku or Fire TV.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan (current prices are shown in Google Play), with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
