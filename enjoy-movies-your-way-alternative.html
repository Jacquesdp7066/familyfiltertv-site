Enjoy Movies Your Way alternative for Android TV and Google TV

Plain answer: Enjoy Movies Your Way is a filtering service for streaming titles, and Family Filter TV is a narrower tool: an app that mutes spoken profanity on Android TV and Google TV while the picture keeps playing. If you want to see what a broader filtering service covers, check Enjoy Movies Your Way's own site. If swearing is the only thing you want gone and you watch on an Android TV or Google TV, Family Filter TV is built for that.

What Enjoy Movies Your Way is

Enjoy Movies Your Way describes itself as a filtering service for streaming platforms. What it filters, which services and countries it supports, which devices it runs on and what it costs are all for it to state, so this page leaves those to its site rather than repeat second-hand figures.

Family Filter TV mutes profanity in speech while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped. It runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone.

Which one fits you

  • Check Enjoy Movies Your Way's site first if you want filtering beyond spoken language, such as violence or nudity. Family Filter TV does not filter those.
  • Check Enjoy Movies Your Way's site if you watch on a device or in a region this page does not cover, and confirm there that the services you use are supported.
  • Choose Family Filter TV if you want swearing muted on an Android TV or Google TV, with Stremio on the TV or by following along while you watch Netflix and other apps.
  • Choose Family Filter TV if you prefer one household setting on your phone (Off, Mild, Family or Strict) that applies to every linked device, with three devices included and up to six.

Questions

Does Family Filter TV filter violence or nudity?

No. It filters spoken profanity only, and it depends on subtitles being available for the title.

Is Family Filter TV a streaming service?

No. It is not a streaming service and provides no films or series. It works alongside the apps you already use.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV. You pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

Which TVs does it run on?

Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes. It does not run on the built-in apps of Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku or Fire TV.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then US$14.99 a year, with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
