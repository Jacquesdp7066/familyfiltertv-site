Family Filter TV: a profanity-muting app, no extra hardware

Family Filter TV is an app for Android TV and Google TV that mutes spoken profanity using subtitles, with a phone to set the level. There is nothing to plug in. If you are looking at other kinds of product, such as a separate device, check that product's own site.

What Family Filter TV is

Family Filter TV mutes profanity in speech while the picture keeps playing, using the subtitles for the title. It runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi.

About Family Filter TV
What it filtersSpoken profanity only, muted while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped. It does not filter violence or nudity.
Where it runsAndroid TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi. It does not run on Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV.
What you needAn Android TV or Google TV device and an Android phone. It depends on subtitles being available for the title.
How it worksWorks with Stremio on the TV. In follow-along mode (version 0.2.21 or newer) you watch Netflix or another app on the Android TV, pick the film or episode on the phone, and the TV mutes the swearing. English titles only for now. Filter levels are Off, Mild, Family and Strict, set once on the phone for every linked device.
Price14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan; current prices are shown in Google Play and charged in local currency. Three devices included, up to six.

Is it right for you?

  • It fits if you already watch on an Android TV or Google TV set or box, and you would rather install an app than add a device.
  • It fits if you want one household setting on your phone that applies to every linked device.
  • Be aware that Family Filter TV filters spoken profanity only, and it depends on subtitles being available for the title. If a title has no subtitles, there is nothing for it to follow.

Questions

Does Family Filter TV need extra hardware?

No. It is an app for Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone.

Does Family Filter TV work on Samsung, LG, Roku or Fire TV?

Yes for Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs, from the Android phone over your home Wi-Fi. Not on Roku or Fire TV. See the page on which TVs work for what does run.

What does Family Filter TV filter?

Spoken profanity only. It mutes the words while the picture keeps playing. It does not filter violence or nudity.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV. You pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan (current prices are shown in Google Play), with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
