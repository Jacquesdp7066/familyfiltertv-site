TVGuardian alternative for Android TV and Google TV

Plain answer: TVGuardian is a separate device that, according to its maker's support site, reads a programme's closed captions and mutes offensive words. Family Filter TV is an app for Android TV and Google TV that mutes spoken profanity using subtitles, with a phone to set the level. If you want a box you plug in and an app is not what you are after, check TVGuardian's site; if you already watch on an Android TV or Google TV, an app may be simpler.

What TVGuardian is

TVGuardian is a profanity filter sold as a hardware device. TVGuardian's support site describes it as reading the hidden closed-caption text of what is on screen, checking it against a dictionary of offensive words and phrases, and muting the sound, with several filter levels. Which connections, televisions and sources it works with is set out on its own site, so check their site for the current list and for pricing.

Family Filter TV is an app. It mutes profanity in speech while the picture keeps playing, using the subtitles for the title, and it runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes with a companion app on an Android phone.

Which one fits you

  • Choose TVGuardian if you watch through a cable box, satellite receiver or DVR and want a dedicated device rather than an app. Its site says its hardware models do not work with online streaming services, and lists a separate product, TVG+, for streaming. Check TVGuardian's site to see which suits your setup.
  • Choose Family Filter TV if you already watch on an Android TV or Google TV set or box, you would rather install an app than add a device, and you want one household setting on your phone that applies to every linked device.
  • Be aware that Family Filter TV filters spoken profanity only, and it depends on subtitles being available for the title. If a title has no subtitles, there is nothing for it to follow.
  • Check TVGuardian's site for anything it does beyond muting words, such as other kinds of content. Family Filter TV does not filter violence or nudity.

Questions

Does Family Filter TV need extra hardware?

No. It is an app for Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone.

Does Family Filter TV work on Samsung, LG, Roku or Fire TV?

Not on the built-in apps of Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku or Fire TV. See the page on which TVs work for what does run.

What does Family Filter TV filter?

Spoken profanity only. It mutes the words while the picture keeps playing. It does not filter violence or nudity.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV: you pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then US$14.99 a year, with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
