Profanity filter browser extension or television: what is the difference?

Plain answer: a browser extension such as Advanced Profanity Filter works inside the browser it is installed in. It can change words on web pages and mute the sound of videos on sites it supports. It does not reach apps on a television. Family Filter TV works on the television side, on Android TV and Google TV, and has an early-beta Windows companion for a computer.

Two different places to filter

Advanced Profanity Filter is an open-source browser extension. Its project page describes it as filtering profanity from web pages in three modes (censor, substitute or remove), with customisable word lists, and as muting audio on videos from supported sites. It lists Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari and Opera as supported browsers and points to its own pages for the full list of supported sites. The project page states no price and links to donation options.

Family Filter TV filters on the television itself. It mutes profanity in speech while the picture keeps playing, on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes with a companion app on an Android phone. There is also an early-beta companion for Windows 10 and 11 PCs that mutes the computer's sound for whatever is playing in a browser or app.

Which one fits you

  • Choose a browser extension if you mostly read or watch on a computer, want to change words on web pages as well as mute video, or want a free, open-source tool. Family Filter TV does not alter web page text.
  • Choose Family Filter TV if the viewing happens on a television, because an extension in a computer browser does not follow you to the apps on the TV.
  • Try the Windows companion if you watch on a PC and want the computer's sound muted for whatever is playing in a browser or app. It is an early beta, so expect rough edges.
  • Both approaches depend on text for the video, such as captions or subtitles, so a title without them gives them nothing to work from.

Questions

Can a browser extension filter my TV?

An extension runs inside the browser where it is installed. Check the extension's own page for any casting instructions; it does not install on apps built into a television.

Does Family Filter TV work in a browser?

Not as an extension. For a PC there is an early-beta Windows 10 and 11 companion that mutes the computer's sound for whatever is playing in a browser or app.

Does Family Filter TV filter web page text?

No. It filters spoken profanity only, muting speech while the picture keeps playing.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV. You pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then US$14.99 a year, with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
