Profanity filter on the television or on a computer: Family Filter TV

Family Filter TV works on the television side, on Android TV and Google TV, and has an early-beta Windows companion for a computer. This page says where it runs and what it does. Other kinds of tool, such as browser extensions, are made by other people; check their own pages for what they do.

Where Family Filter TV runs

Family Filter TV filters on the television itself. It mutes profanity in speech while the picture keeps playing, on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi. There is also an early-beta companion for Windows 10 and 11 PCs that mutes the computer's sound for whatever is playing in a browser or app.

About Family Filter TV
What it filtersSpoken profanity only, muted while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped. It does not filter web page text, violence or nudity.
Where it runsAndroid TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi. It does not run on Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV. Plus an early-beta companion for Windows 10 and 11 PCs.
What you needAn Android TV or Google TV device and an Android phone. It depends on subtitles being available for the title.
How it worksWorks with Stremio on the TV. In follow-along mode (version 0.2.21 or newer) you watch Netflix or another app on the Android TV, pick the film or episode on the phone, and the TV mutes the swearing. English titles only for now. Filter levels are Off, Mild, Family and Strict, set once on the phone for every linked device.
Price14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan; current prices are shown in Google Play and charged in local currency. Three devices included, up to six.

Which one fits you

  • Family Filter TV fits if the viewing happens on a television, on Android TV or Google TV, or on a Samsung or LG smart TV with an Android phone.
  • Try the Windows companion if you watch on a PC and want the computer's sound muted for whatever is playing in a browser or app. It is an early beta, so expect rough edges.
  • It does not change words on web pages. If you want that, it is not the tool for it.
  • It depends on subtitles being available, so a title without them gives it nothing to work from.

Questions

Does Family Filter TV work in a browser?

Not as an extension. For a PC there is an early-beta Windows 10 and 11 companion that mutes the computer's sound for whatever is playing in a browser or app.

Does Family Filter TV filter web page text?

No. It filters spoken profanity only, muting speech while the picture keeps playing.

Does it run on a television?

Yes, on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV. You pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan (current prices are shown in Google Play), with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
