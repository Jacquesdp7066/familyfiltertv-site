What the app is

FamilyFilterTV is a video player that registers as an external player for Stremio. It plays streams Stremio hands to it and mutes its own audio output during subtitle lines that match a profanity-filter profile you choose. It does not host, index, source, or redistribute any video content itself — all streams and subtitles come from Stremio and its add-ons.

Accuracy of filtering

Filtering quality depends entirely on the accuracy and availability of subtitles supplied by Stremio's add-ons or embedded in the video file. FamilyFilterTV does not generate, verify, or guarantee the correctness of any subtitle, and cannot filter content for which no matching subtitle can be found. Use your own judgement about what is appropriate for your household — this app is a filtering aid, not a guarantee of content suitability.
