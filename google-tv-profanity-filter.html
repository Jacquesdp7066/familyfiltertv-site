Profanity filter for Google TV

Family Filter TV works on Google TV. Google TV sets, sticks and boxes run the same system as Android TV, so the app works the same way. Install it on the TV and on an Android phone, link them, and it mutes spoken profanity while the picture keeps playing. It uses the film’s subtitles, so muting is best effort and a line may slip through.

Is my TV a Google TV?

Press Home on your remote. If you see Google TV, or the Google Play Store among the apps, Family Filter TV works on that TV.

The system matters, not the brand. Some brands sell more than one kind of TV. Our page Will it work on my TV? has the brand-by-brand answer.

Do you have a TV that cannot run the app? A Google TV or Android TV streaming stick or box in an HDMI port is the simplest way today.

What you need

  • A Google TV or Android TV set, stick or box.
  • An Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) with the app.
  • Family Filter TV version 0.2.21 or newer on both, for follow-along with other apps. If Google Play still offers an older version, use the direct download.
  • Subtitles for the film or episode. The app tells you when it cannot find them.

Set it up

  1. Install Family Filter TV on the TV from Google Play. If the Play Store on your TV does not show it, use the direct install.

  2. Install it on your phone, open it and sign in or create your account.

  3. Link the TV. The TV shows a short code. On the phone, tap Account, then Link a device, and type the code.

  4. Let the TV see what is playing. On the TV, choose Follow along, then Open settings, and switch Family Filter TV on. You only do this once.

  5. Start a film. Press play on the TV. On the phone, search for the same title and tap Start following.

The setup guide has every tap.

What it works with

Family Filter TV works with Stremio. It also follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Google TV. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix. You watch with your own accounts. We do not provide films or series.

What to expect

  • The picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut or skipped.
  • You choose a level: Off, Mild, Family or Strict.
  • It mutes speech only. It does not filter violence or nudity.
  • It works for English titles for now.
  • It does not work with the built-in apps of Samsung, LG and other non-Android TVs.

Questions

Does Family Filter TV work on Google TV?

Yes. Google TV runs the same system as Android TV, and Family Filter TV runs on both.

How do I know if my TV is a Google TV?

Press Home on the remote. If you see Google TV, or the Google Play Store among the apps, it works.

Does it work on a Google TV streaming stick or box?

Yes. Sticks and boxes work the same as a Google TV set. They also give a simple option for a TV that cannot run the app itself.

Do I need a phone?

Yes. Install the app on an Android phone too. The phone is where you sign in, link the TV and choose the title to follow.

Will it mute swearing in the Netflix app on Google TV?

Yes, it can follow along with Netflix and other apps on a Google TV, with version 0.2.21 or newer. Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.
