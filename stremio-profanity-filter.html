Profanity filtering for Stremio on Android TV

Start watching in Stremio and use Family Filter TV for a cleaner language experience. The picture keeps playing while unwanted language is automatically muted.

Install Family Filter TV Setup guide

Simple to use

  1. Install both apps

    Install Stremio and Family Filter TV on your Android TV or TV box.

  2. Choose Family Filter TV for playback

    Set it once from Stremio and Android can remember your choice.

  3. Choose your filter level

    Use off, mild, family or strict from the Family Filter TV app.

  4. Watch

    Supported titles play with unwanted language automatically muted.

Know before you start

Filtering availability varies by title. Family Filter TV tells you when a title cannot be filtered. It is not a streaming library and does not provide content or subscription access.
