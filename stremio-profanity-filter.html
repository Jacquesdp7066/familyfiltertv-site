Use a profanity filter with Stremio on Android TV

Family Filter TV works as Stremio's external player. Stremio starts the title, Android hands the stream to Family Filter TV, and the player uses subtitle timing to mute profane speech.

Install Family Filter TV Full setup guide

How it works

  1. Start your title in Stremio

    Choose the film or episode as normal.

  2. Choose External player

    Open Stremio's Player menu and choose External. When Android asks which app to use, choose Family Filter TV.

  3. Family Filter TV plays it

    The stream now plays inside Family Filter TV, where subtitle lines can be timed against playback.

  4. Profane lines are muted

    The picture continues normally. Only the audio is briefly muted for matched subtitle lines.

Why an external player?

Android apps cannot normally control the audio inside another app's private player. By receiving the stream as the external player, Family Filter TV controls its own playback and can mute its own audio accurately.

What happens when subtitles are missing?

Filtering depends on subtitle timing. If no usable subtitles are found, Family Filter TV shows that filtering is unavailable for that title rather than pretending the audio is being filtered.
