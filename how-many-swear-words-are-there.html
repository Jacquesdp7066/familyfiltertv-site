How many swear words are there?

TL;DR: There is no official count of swear words. The English lists we checked hold 279 to 1,795 entries, and in the 1,591 films we analysed on 8 November 2026, ten word forms make up 69% of all flagged words. There is no official number. The size of a swear word list depends on who wrote it and what they decided to count. The English lists we checked on 8 November 2026 run from 279 entries to 1,795. The better question is how few words do most of the work, and in the films we analysed the answer is about ten.

Three real lists, three different numbers

Nobody governs English, so nobody decides which words are swear words. What exists are lists that people and software projects have written for their own purposes. Here are three, counted on 8 November 2026.

The gap is mostly about what the list is for. A list built to catch every possible rude word, like cuss, includes words that are only sometimes rude. A list built to mute a film, like ours, has to be shorter, because every extra entry risks muting an ordinary sentence.

Why there is no single answer

  • What counts as a swear word. Strong profanity, slurs, sexual terms, crude words, mild words such as damn, and religious exclamations such as oh my god are all called swear words by someone. Some people count only the first.
  • Endings and compounds. Fuck, fucked, fucking and motherfucker are one entry on one list and four on another. Our matcher counts a word with common endings such as -s, -ed and -ing as the same entry.
  • Phrases. Some lists include whole phrases, and some words are fine alone but rude in company.
  • Place and time. A word that is mild in one country can be among the worst in another, and the list changes as language does.

A few words do most of the work

A long list does not mean a long vocabulary in practice. On 8 November 2026 we counted every flagged word in the English subtitles of 1,591 films, 106,781 flagged words in all. Only 259 different word forms ever showed up, and most of the weight sits in a few of them.

The ten most common forms are not all strong. Four are the F-word in different endings or the S-word, and the other six are mild or religious: words such as hell, damn, ass, oh my god, oh god and goddamn. That is why a film can have a high flagged-word count without sounding extreme. The famous seven

The best-known number is seven. George Carlin’s 1972 routine “Seven Dirty Words” named the seven words he said you couldn’t say on American radio and television. A recording of it led to the US Supreme Court case FCC v. Pacifica Foundation in 1978. It was a comedy bit, not a census, but it is why many people think of swearing as a short list of words.

Watching with the swearing muted

If you like films and shows but would rather not hear the language, Family Filter TV mutes the swear words it finds in the subtitles, using the list described above, while the picture keeps playing. It works on an Android TV or Google TV with supported Stremio playback, and it follows along with Netflix and other apps. It covers spoken language only, English titles for now, and muting is best effort, so a word may slip through. It does not filter violence or nudity.

Questions

How many swear words are there?

There is no official number, because it depends on what you count. Three English lists we checked on 8 November 2026 hold 279 entries (Family Filter TV's own list), 403 (the open-source List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words) and 1,795 (the cuss word list, version 2.2.0, which rates each entry as likely, maybe or unlikely to be profane). In practice a handful of words do most of the work: in the 1,591 films we analysed on 8 November 2026, the 10 most common flagged forms make up 69% of all flagged words.

How many curse words are there?

The same answer as for swear words: no official figure. "Curse words", "swear words" and "profanity" are used loosely, and lists differ on whether they include slurs, sexual terms, mild words like damn and religious exclamations like oh my god. Published English lists run from a few hundred entries to about 1,800.

How many swear words are there in the English language?

Nobody has counted them in a way everyone accepts. English has no body that decides which words are taboo, and the answer changes with the list and with how you treat endings and compounds: a list that counts fuck, fucked, fucking and motherfucker as four entries gives a bigger number than one that counts them as one. The lists we checked range from 279 to 1,795 entries.

How many dirty words did George Carlin list?

Seven. George Carlin's 1972 routine "Seven Dirty Words" named the words he said could not be said on American broadcast radio and television. A recording of it led to the US Supreme Court case FCC v. Pacifica Foundation in 1978. It was a comedy routine, not a count of all swear words, and the number of words that bother people today is much larger.

Which swear words are used most in films?

In the 1,591 films whose English subtitles we analysed on 8 November 2026, the F-word in its different endings and the S-word are the most common strong words, but mild and religious language is just as common: damn-type words, hell, ass, oh my god, oh god and goddamn all sit in the top ten flagged forms. The top 5 forms make up 55% of all flagged words, the top 10 make up 69% and the top 20 make up 81%.

Can I watch a film without hearing the swearing?

On an Android TV or Google TV, yes. Family Filter TV mutes the swear words it finds in the subtitles while the picture keeps playing, using the same word list described on this page. It covers spoken language only, English titles for now, and muting is best effort, so a word may slip through.
