TikTok publisher

This page is only for the Family Filter TV team. It connects our own TikTok account so a finished video can be sent to it as a draft.

We ask for two permissions: user.info.basic (to show which account is connected) and video.upload (to put one video in that account's drafts). We never post without the account owner opening the draft in TikTok and publishing it. No other TikTok user's data is read or stored.
