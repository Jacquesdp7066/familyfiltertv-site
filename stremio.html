Language Ratings for Stremio

A free add-on for Stremio that shows how much swearing is in a film or episode — counted from its English subtitles — right on the streams screen, before you press play.

Add to Stremio

If the button above doesn't do anything — some browsers and TVs block app links — open Stremio, go to Add-ons, and paste this into the search box at the top:

https://addon.familyfiltertv.com/manifest.json

What you'll see

Once it's added, open any film or episode in Stremio and look at the streams list. Language Ratings adds one line, alongside whatever else you have installed:

Language rating

Strong · 41 words in 33 lines
Muted lines by profile: Mild 12 · Family 29 · Strict 33
Watch it muted with Family Filter TV — familyfiltertv.com/stremio

Tap it and you'll land back on this page — it isn't a video, just a note. If a title has no English subtitles available anywhere, it says so plainly instead of staying silent.

What the labels mean

Every count is against the Family Filter TV app's own filter profiles — Mild, Family, and Strict, from least to most muted. A title is labelled by how many lines each profile would mute:

LabelWhat it means
CleanThe Strict profile mutes nothing.
MildThe Mild profile mutes nothing, and Strict mutes 5 lines or fewer.
ModerateThe Mild profile mutes some lines, or Strict mutes 6–30.
StrongThe Mild profile mutes 10 or more lines, or Strict mutes more than 30.

How it's counted, and its limits

Counts come from a title's English subtitles only, using the same word-matching Family Filter TV's own app uses to decide what to mute. That means:

  • No English subtitle track, no count — the entry says so instead of guessing.
  • Different cuts of the same film (theatrical vs. extended, different releases) can have different subtitle timing, so a count reflects the specific release it was measured on, not every version that exists.
  • Only language is counted here. Violence and nudity are not measured by this add-on or by the Family Filter TV app.
  • It's a count, not a judgement — the same numbers, shown plainly, so you can decide for yourself before you press play.

Counts are made from OpenSubtitles.com subtitles. No subtitle text is stored or shown anywhere — only the counts.

Want the language actually muted, not just counted?

That's what the Family Filter TV app does: it plays alongside Stremio as an external player and mutes the audio live, for the exact seconds a line is profane, while the picture keeps playing.
