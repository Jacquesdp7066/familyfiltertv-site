Watch Stremio without the swearing

FamilyFilterTV is an Android app — TV and phone — that plugs into Stremio as its external video player. Start anything in Stremio, hand it to FamilyFilterTV, and it plays the stream normally but mutes its own audio for the exact seconds a subtitle line is profane. Nothing is cut, blurred, or skipped — the picture keeps playing, the sound just drops out and comes back.

What it does
  • Registers as an external player Stremio can hand a stream to.
  • Reads subtitles from the video's own text track, or fetches them from Stremio's free OpenSubtitles add-on when the video doesn't have any.
  • Mutes its own audio output for the duration of any subtitle line that matches your chosen filter strength (Off / Mild / Family / Strict). It never edits, uploads, or redistributes the video or subtitle file — filtering happens locally, live, during playback.
  • Works on Android TV (D-pad, big-screen layout) and on Android phones (touch).
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads in this version.

Setting it up with Stremio

  1. Install Stremio and FamilyFilterTV on the same Android device.
  2. Open FamilyFilterTV once from the launcher so you can pick a filter profile (default Family) and, optionally, whether subtitles are drawn on screen — muting works either way.
  3. In Stremio, start any title, open the Player menu on the control bar (the picture-in-frame icon) and choose External.
  4. Android shows an "Open with" chooser — pick FamilyFilterTV and tap Always so Stremio goes straight to it from then on.
  5. Play as normal. A small banner at the top of the screen for the first few seconds shows where the subtitles came from, or says so plainly if none were found — filtering only works where subtitles exist for that title.

Known limits

  • Some phones and boxes don't decode 10-bit HEVC video in hardware — playback may stutter or fail on those streams regardless of FamilyFilterTV.
  • Filtering depends entirely on a matching subtitle track being available. If Stremio's add-ons can't find subtitles for a title, FamilyFilterTV plays it unfiltered and says so on screen.
