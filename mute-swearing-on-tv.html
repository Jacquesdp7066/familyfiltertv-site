How to mute swearing on TV without cutting the video

Family Filter TV uses subtitle timing to know when a profane line is being spoken. The player briefly mutes its own audio, then restores it while the video keeps playing normally.

Try Family Filter TV Android TV guide

The basic idea

  • The video keeps playing.
  • The app reads the active subtitle text and timing.
  • Profane lines are matched against the selected filter level.
  • Audio is muted for the matched interval.
  • Audio returns immediately afterwards.

What it does not do

Family Filter TV does not cut scenes, blur the picture, skip violence or nudity, or edit the underlying video. Its current job is language filtering by muting speech.

Why subtitles matter

Subtitles provide both the words and their timing. That lets the player schedule a mute around the spoken line. A title without usable subtitles cannot currently be filtered, and the app reports that clearly on screen.

Where it works today

The current supported workflow is Android TV or an Android TV box with Stremio handing playback to Family Filter TV as the external player.
