Family Filter TV for Stremio and Android TV households

Family Filter TV is an app that mutes spoken profanity on Android TV and Google TV while the picture keeps playing. This page says what it does and does not do, so you can decide whether it fits your household. Other movie filtering services exist, such as VidAngel and ClearPlay; they publish their own details, so check their sites.

What Family Filter TV does

  • It mutes spoken profanity, using the subtitles for the title, while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped.
  • It works with Stremio on the TV, and it can follow along while you watch Netflix and other apps on an Android TV or Google TV (English titles only for now).
  • It runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi.
  • You set the filter level once on the phone (Off, Mild, Family or Strict) and it applies to every linked device. Three devices are included, up to six.
  • It is not restricted to one country. It depends on subtitles being available for the title.

What it does not do

  • It does not filter violence or nudity. Language is the only thing it changes.
  • Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through.
  • It does not work with DStv, and it does not run on Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV.
  • It is not a streaming service and provides no films or series. You watch with your own accounts.

Is it right for you?

If you mainly want swearing muted, and you watch through Stremio or other apps on an Android TV or Google TV, Family Filter TV is built for that. If you want more than language taken out, or you watch on a device it does not run on, look at other tools. For the questions worth asking of any filtering service, see choosing a movie filtering service.

Questions

Is Family Filter TV affiliated with VidAngel or ClearPlay?

No. Family Filter TV is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to VidAngel, ClearPlay or any streaming service.

What does Family Filter TV filter?

Spoken profanity only. It mutes the words while the picture keeps playing. It does not filter violence or nudity.

Does it work with Stremio?

Yes, on the TV. Family Filter TV mutes profanity in Stremio playback using the subtitles for the title.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV. You pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan (current prices are shown in Google Play), with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
