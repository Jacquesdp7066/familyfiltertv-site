Will it work on my TV?

It depends on the system your TV runs, not the brand. If your TV has the Google Play Store, yes.

How to tell in 10 seconds

Press Home on your TV remote. If you see Google TV, or the Google Play Store among the apps, Family Filter TV works on that TV.

Brands often sell more than one kind of TV, so check the Home screen test above for your own set. Brand names belong to their owners. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with them.

I have a Samsung or LG

The simplest way today is a Google TV or Android TV streaming stick or box in an HDMI port of your TV. Watch through it, and Family Filter TV works the same as on any Google TV.

We are honest about the limit: the built-in Samsung and LG apps cannot be filtered, because those TVs do not let one app mute another. We are looking at other ways for these TVs, but we cannot promise anything.

What else you need

  • An Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) with the Family Filter TV app.
  • One Family Filter TV account.
  • Subtitles for the film or episode. The app tells you when it cannot find them.
