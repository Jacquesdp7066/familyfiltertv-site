Knocked Up (2007) parents guide: swearing and language

A one-night encounter between a career-focused journalist and an easygoing slacker leads to an unplanned pregnancy and a relationship neither of them is quite ready for. It's a well-known R-rated comedy, and its reputation for blunt, constant profanity is exactly why people search for its language content before watching.

This guide covers spoken language only. It does not rate violence, nudity or frightening scenes.

How much swearing is in Knocked Up?

We counted 377 flagged words in the English subtitles of Knocked Up: 206 strong profanity, 27 mild profanity, 68 religious exclamations, 40 crude language, 30 explicit sexual language, 4 suggestive language, 2 slurs.

What Family Filter TV can filter

  • Profanity, matched against your chosen filter profile (mild, family or strict).
  • Subtitle-driven mute events — the sound briefly drops on a matching line and returns right after.

How filtering works

Open the title through a supported playback route such as Stremio. Family Filter TV analyses subtitle cues against your household filter settings and automatically mutes matching dialogue during playback. The picture keeps playing — only the sound briefly drops.
