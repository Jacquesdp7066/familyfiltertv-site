Zack Snyder's Justice League (2021) parents guide: swearing and language

This extended cut reassembles Batman, Wonder Woman and the rest of the League to stop an invading force before Superman's absence leaves Earth defenceless. At four hours, it has considerably more dialogue than the theatrical cut, which is part of why its language content differs from the version most people remember.

This guide covers spoken language only. It does not rate violence, nudity or frightening scenes.

How Zack Snyder's Justice League compares

With the family filter on, 23 of 2808 subtitle lines are muted (0.8%), so the rest of the dialogue plays as normal.

What Family Filter TV can filter

  • Profanity, matched against your chosen filter profile (mild, family or strict).
  • Subtitle-driven mute events — the sound briefly drops on a matching line and returns right after.

How filtering works

Open the title through a supported playback route such as Stremio. Family Filter TV analyses subtitle cues against your household filter settings and automatically mutes matching dialogue during playback. The picture keeps playing — only the sound briefly drops.
