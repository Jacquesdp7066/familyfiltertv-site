Holes (2003) parents guide: swearing and language

A teenager wrongly sent to a brutal juvenile detention camp starts digging holes in the desert for reasons nobody will explain, slowly uncovering a decades-old family mystery. It's a popular tween read-then-watch pick, and schools and parents alike often want to know what's in it before a group viewing.

This guide covers spoken language only. It does not rate violence, nudity or frightening scenes.
