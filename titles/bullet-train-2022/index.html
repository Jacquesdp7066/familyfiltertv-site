Bullet Train (2022) parents guide: swearing and language

A reluctant assassin boards a bullet train for what should be a simple job, only to discover several other killers on board with overlapping, conflicting missions. The action-comedy's banter is blunt and frequently profane, consistent with its R rating and its ensemble of foul-mouthed killers.

This guide covers spoken language only. It does not rate violence, nudity or frightening scenes.

How much swearing is in Bullet Train?

We counted 264 flagged words in the English subtitles of Bullet Train: 203 strong profanity, 27 mild profanity, 18 religious exclamations, 11 crude language, 5 explicit sexual language.
