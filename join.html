How it works

  1. Install it on your TV

    Get it from Google Play on your Android TV or Google TV, then link your household with the short code the TV shows.

  2. Choose how strict it is

    Off, mild, family or strict, set once and followed by every linked TV in the house.

  3. Press play

    Start a title in Stremio and pick Family Filter TV as the player: that is where it is most accurate. For Netflix and other apps on Android TV it follows along instead (English titles), and it works better on some titles than others.

It mutes the words and leaves the picture alone. It is for adults who like a film or show but not the language, and it does not change what is on screen, so it is not a way to make a film suitable for children. See how it works and what it can miss.

Once you're set up, you can invite friends of your own: 10 weeks free for every friend who subscribes, up to five friends a year.
