Family Filter TV on Android TV and Google TV (not Apple TV)

Family Filter TV mutes spoken profanity on Android TV and Google TV while the picture keeps playing, with an Android phone as the companion. It does not run on Apple TV. If Apple TV is what you own, this is not the app for it.

What Family Filter TV runs on

Family Filter TV runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone, and it mutes spoken profanity while the picture keeps playing. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi. It does not run on Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV.

About Family Filter TV
What it filtersSpoken profanity only, muted while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped. It does not filter violence or nudity.
What you needAn Android TV or Google TV device and an Android phone. It depends on subtitles being available for the title.
How it worksWorks with Stremio on the TV. In follow-along mode (version 0.2.21 or newer) you watch Netflix or another app on the Android TV, pick the film or episode on the phone, and the TV mutes the swearing. English titles only for now. Filter levels are Off, Mild, Family and Strict, set once on the phone for every linked device.
Price14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan; current prices are shown in Google Play and charged in local currency. Three devices included, up to six.

Questions

Does Family Filter TV run on Apple TV?

No. It runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone.

Do I need an Android phone?

Yes. The companion app on an Android phone sets the filter level and, in follow-along mode, is where you pick the film or episode.

Does it work with Netflix?

Family Filter TV follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on your Android TV. You pick the film or episode on the phone and the TV mutes the swearing. This needs version 0.2.21 or newer, and English titles only for now. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix. If Google Play still offers an older version, the newer one is on the download page.

Does it work with Stremio?

Yes, on the TV. Family Filter TV mutes profanity in Stremio playback using the subtitles for the title.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan (current prices are shown in Google Play), with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.
