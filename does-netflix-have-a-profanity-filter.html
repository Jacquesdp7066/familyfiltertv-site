Muting swearing on Netflix on an Android TV or Google TV

Netflix publishes its own parental controls; see its Help Center for what they do today. Family Filter TV is a separate app, not affiliated with Netflix, that mutes swearing while Netflix keeps playing on an Android TV or Google TV.

Netflix's own controls

Netflix's Help Center describes a maturity level you set for each profile, Kids profiles, and the option to block specific shows and movies on a profile. Read Netflix Help Center, parental controls and maturity ratings for the current details. Family Filter TV does not replace or change any of them.

Where Family Filter TV fits

Family Filter TV is an app that runs on the TV next to the Netflix app. You start the film or episode in Netflix on the TV, pick the same title on your Android phone, and the TV mutes the swearing it finds in the subtitles while the picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped, and when the episode ends the TV follows the next one by itself. You choose how strict the filter is: off, mild, family or strict.

After a 14-day free trial you choose a 30-day or 1-year plan; current prices are shown in Google Play, in your local currency, and three devices are included. You watch with your own Netflix account, because Family Filter TV does not provide films or series.

The step-by-step version is on how to mute swearing on Netflix.

What to expect, and what it does not do

  • Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through.
  • It works for English titles for now, and only when subtitles are found for the title.
  • It does not filter violence or nudity. Language is the only thing it changes.
  • It works on Android TV and Google TV, and it needs an Android phone as well. On Samsung and LG smart TVs the Android phone mutes the TV over your home Wi-Fi, including the Netflix app built into the TV.
  • It needs Family Filter TV version 0.2.21 or newer on the TV and the phone.

Questions

Can I mute swearing on Netflix on my TV?

Yes, on an Android TV or Google TV. Family Filter TV runs on the TV next to the Netflix app and mutes the sound for the swear words it finds in the subtitles, while the picture keeps playing. Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.

Where can I find Netflix's own controls?

In Netflix's Help Center at help.netflix.com, under parental controls and maturity ratings. Check it for what the controls do today.

Does Family Filter TV filter violence or nudity on Netflix?

No. Family Filter TV mutes spoken profanity only. It does not filter violence or nudity, and it works for English titles for now.

Does Family Filter TV change Netflix or the video?

No. It does not change the Netflix app, cut scenes or provide any films or series. You watch with your own Netflix account, and Family Filter TV only lowers the TV sound for a moment.

Is Family Filter TV affiliated with Netflix?

No. Family Filter TV is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Netflix or any other streaming service.
