Family Filter TV: the facts

Family Filter TV is a profanity filter for Android TV, Android TV boxes and Android phones that automatically mutes unwanted language while the picture keeps playing.

Last updated 10 October 2026

What it is

NameFamily Filter TV (also written FamilyFilterTV)
In one sentenceA profanity filter for Android TV, Android TV boxes and Android phones that automatically mutes unwanted language while the picture keeps playing.
Websitefamilyfiltertv.com
Filter levelsOff, mild, family or strict. The filter is set once on a parent's phone and every linked device follows it.

What it does

  • Mutes profanity in speech. Only the sound drops, for the length of that line, and then comes straight back.
  • Works with supported playback you start in Stremio.
  • Follows along while you watch Netflix and other apps on an Android TV or Google TV: you pick the same title on an Android phone and the TV mutes the swearing. It needs version 0.2.21 or newer, English titles only for now. Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through. Family Filter TV is not affiliated with Netflix.
  • Links a television by the short code it shows on screen, typed into the phone app.
  • Has an early-beta companion for Windows 10 and 11 that mutes the computer's sound while a flagged word is spoken.

What it does not do

  • It does not filter violence or nudity.
  • It does not cut, skip or blur the video.
  • It is not a streaming library and does not provide films, series or subscription access.
  • It only mutes when subtitles are found for the title. Filtering availability varies by title, and the app tells the viewer when filtering is unavailable.
  • It does not work with the built-in apps of Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Hisense VIDAA and other non-Android TVs. Roku TVs and Amazon Fire TV are not supported at this time.
  • It does not filter DStv.

Where it runs

Runs onAndroid TVs and Android TV boxes (including Google TV), Android phones, and Windows 10 or 11 as an early beta.
Does not run onThe built-in apps of Samsung and LG TVs, Hisense sets running VIDAA, Roku TVs and Amazon Fire TV. A Google TV or Android TV streaming stick or box plugged into the TV's HDMI port works.
How to tellPress Home on the TV remote. If you see Google TV, or the Google Play Store among the apps, it works. See Will it work on my TV?

What you need

  • A Family Filter TV account.
  • An Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) with the Family Filter TV app.
  • Subtitles for the film or episode.
  • For follow along in Netflix and other apps: Family Filter TV on an Android TV or Google TV device and on an Android phone.

Versions and where to get them

Google PlayFamily Filter TV on Google Play (package com.familyfiltertv.app).
Direct install (APK)For TV boxes without Google Play. The current version is on the download page.
Follow along needsVersion 0.2.21 or newer. If Google Play still offers an older version, install from the download page.
WindowsEarly beta for Windows 10 and 11. See the Windows section of the download page.
Language RatingsA separate, free Stremio add-on that counts the swearing in a film from its English subtitles. It only counts; it does not mute. See Language Ratings for Stremio.

Price

Annual priceUS$14.99 a year, with the first 14 days free. Google Play shows and charges the price in your own currency.
DevicesThree devices are included on one account, and up to six can be linked. A 4th, 5th or 6th device adds US$3.09, US$5.99 or US$8.99 a year.
Inviting friendsEvery friend who subscribes through your link earns you 10 weeks free. Your friend gets 30 days free instead of the usual 14.
