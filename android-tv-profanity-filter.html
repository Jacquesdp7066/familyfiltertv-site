Profanity filter for Android TV

Watch together without unwanted language taking over movie night. Family Filter TV automatically mutes profanity while the picture keeps playing.

Get it on Google Play See the setup

Built for the TV your family already watches

  • Runs on Android TV and Android TV boxes.
  • Works with supported Stremio playback.
  • Choose off, mild, family or strict filtering.
  • Manage the household filter from your Android phone.
  • Three devices are included, with support for up to six.

What watching feels like

The picture keeps playing normally. When unwanted language is filtered, the sound briefly drops and returns automatically. Nothing is cut from the video.

Current limits

Filtering availability varies by title. Family Filter TV tells you when filtering is unavailable. It currently filters profanity in speech; violence and nudity are not filtered.

Works with Stremio

Family Filter TV is designed for supported Stremio playback on Android TV. It is an independent app and does not provide or unlock films, series or subscription services.
