Profanity filter for Android TV

Family Filter TV runs on Android TV and Android TV boxes. When you start a title in Stremio and hand playback to Family Filter TV, it reads subtitle timing and briefly mutes the audio when a profane line is spoken.

Get it on Google Play See the setup

What it does

  • Mutes profanity in speech using subtitle timing.
  • Keeps the video playing instead of cutting or skipping scenes.
  • Lets you choose off, mild, family or strict filtering.
  • Lets a parent change the household filter from an Android phone.
  • Supports Android TVs, Android TV boxes and Android phones.

What you need

  1. An Android TV or Android TV box

    Install Family Filter TV from Google Play, or use the direct-install option on supported boxes without Play.

  2. Stremio for playback

    Start the film or episode in Stremio, then choose Family Filter TV as the external player.

  3. Subtitles

    The filter needs a usable subtitle track to know when a profane line is spoken. If no subtitles are available, the app tells you and plays unfiltered.

Current limits

Family Filter TV is a player, not a streaming library. It does not open or unlock subscription streaming services and it cannot reach audio inside another app's own player. Today the supported filtering workflow is playback handed over from Stremio.
