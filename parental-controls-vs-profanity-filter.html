Parental controls vs a profanity filter

Parental controls decide which titles can be played, usually by age rating. A profanity filter works after that, inside a title you have allowed, and mutes the swear words. They do different jobs. Parental controls do not remove words from a film. A filter does not block a film. Many families use both.

What parental controls do

TV and app parental controls block by age rating. You set a limit, such as no films above a chosen rating. Often you set a PIN, too.

They are useful. They keep young children from starting adult titles. They also help when you are not in the room.

But they have limits:

  • A rating is one label for a whole film. It does not tell you how many swear words there are.
  • Rating systems differ between countries and apps.
  • Once a title is allowed, everything in it is allowed.
  • They are set separately on each TV, app and account.

What a profanity filter does

A profanity filter does not decide what can be played. It mutes spoken swearing in a title you have allowed. The picture keeps playing. Nothing is cut or skipped.

Family Filter TV has four levels: Off, Mild, Family and Strict. You pick how strict it is for your household. It mutes speech only. It does not filter violence or nudity.

It also has limits. It depends on subtitles. Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through. It does not block anything, so a child can still start any title the TV allows.

Using both together

  1. Use parental controls to set which ratings can start on each screen.
  2. Use a filter for the swearing inside titles you have allowed.

Questions

What is the difference between parental controls and a profanity filter?

Parental controls block titles by age rating. A profanity filter mutes swear words inside a title you allow.

Do parental controls stop swearing?

No. A film that passes the rating limit plays in full, including any swearing.

Does a profanity filter block films?

No. Family Filter TV does not block anything. It mutes spoken profanity while the picture keeps playing.

Should I use both?

Many families do. Parental controls decide what can start. A filter handles the language in titles you allow.

Does Family Filter TV change Stremio’s settings?

No. Stremio’s settings are separate. Family Filter TV works alongside Stremio and is not affiliated with it.

Does a filter also remove violence or nudity?

No. Family Filter TV mutes speech only.
