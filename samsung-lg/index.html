Samsung and LG TVs: early access

Samsung and LG: early access, muted from your phone. Your Android phone follows the film and mutes the TV over your home Wi-Fi. Nothing is installed on the TV.

Early access means early testing

This is new and is in early testing with one family. We have not proven it on every Samsung or LG model, and it may not work on yours yet. It is not in the public Google Play release of the app yet. If you have a Samsung or LG TV and would like to try it, write to jacquesjdupreez@gmail.com.

How it works

On Android TV and Google TV, Family Filter TV runs on the TV. Samsung and LG TVs do not let one app mute another, so here the phone does the work. You pick the film or episode on your phone, and the app mutes the TV’s sound for the flagged words, then brings the sound straight back. The picture keeps playing. Because the phone mutes the TV itself, it works with any app on the TV, Netflix and the rest included.

If you mute the TV with your remote, the app leaves your mute alone. It only unmutes a mute it made, and it unmutes if you pause, skip, stop, or the connection is lost.

What you need

  • A Samsung or LG smart TV, switched on.
  • An Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer) with the Family Filter TV app and your account.
  • The phone on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  • Subtitles for the film or episode. The app tells you when it cannot find them.
  • One setting on the TV, below.

What to switch on on the TV

Menu names change from year to year. Check on your set, menu names vary by year.

Samsung

Settings → General → External Device Manager → Device Connect Manager → Access Notification → “First time only”.

Check on your set, menu names vary by year.

LG

Settings → Network → LG Connect Apps. On older sets it is under General → Mobile App.

Check on your set, menu names vary by year.

LG sets use an encrypted connection by default. The app has an “older unencrypted connection” switch for older sets. It is off unless you turn it on.

How pairing looks

  1. On the phone, open the Follow screen. The app looks for TVs on your Wi-Fi. Pick yours, or type its address if it is not listed.
  2. The first time, the TV shows a prompt asking whether to allow the phone. Press Allow with the remote. The app gives you 30 seconds.
  3. The phone remembers the TV, so you pair once per phone. Then follow a title as usual.

The pairing details stay on your phone. They are not sent to us.

The limits, plainly

  • Same Wi-Fi. The phone has to stay on the same Wi-Fi as the TV the whole time. If it cannot reach the TV, the app says so and unmutes.
  • The phone screen stays on for now. Keep the phone awake and with you while you watch.
  • Samsung needs a set that reports whether it is muted. Some Samsung models do not. If yours does not, the app says “This TV can’t be muted from the phone yet” and does not guess, because a wrong guess could leave your TV muted.
  • English titles only for now, as on Android TV.
  • It filters profanity in speech. Violence and nudity are not filtered.
  • Roku, Fire TV, Hisense VIDAA and Apple TV are not covered by this.

Questions

Can Family Filter TV mute a Samsung or LG TV?

Early access, in early testing. Your Android phone follows the film and mutes the TV over your home Wi-Fi. Nothing is installed on the TV. We have not yet proven it on every model. If the app says “This TV can’t be muted from the phone yet”, that Samsung set cannot report whether it is muted, so it is not supported yet.

Does it work with Netflix and other apps on a Samsung or LG TV?

It is designed to. The phone mutes the TV’s sound, so it is meant to work with whatever app is playing on the TV, including Netflix. This is early access and not proven on every model.

What do I need for a Samsung or LG TV?

An Android phone with Family Filter TV on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, a Family Filter TV account, and a TV setting switched on that lets the phone talk to it. For now the phone screen stays on while you watch.
