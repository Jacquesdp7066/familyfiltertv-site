What's new in Family Filter TV 1.1.13

Version 1.1.13 is the current Android release on Google Play. It made following a title steadier and added a way to delete your account from inside the app.

Released 9 November 2026. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Following a title is steadier

  • It stays on through rewinds. Following a title now stays on when you rewind. See how to mute swearing on Netflix for how follow along works.
  • It corrects big timing gaps by itself. When the timing is far out from the dialogue, the app corrects it without you doing anything.
  • A Follow again button. There is now a Follow again button to start following the title again.

Muting is best effort, so a line may still slip through, and follow along works with English titles only for now. See the support page for the known limits.

Delete your account inside the app

You can now delete your account from the Android app: open the Account tab and tap Delete account. It removes your household, devices and settings. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription, so cancel that first. The full steps, what is deleted and what is kept are on the delete your account page.

How to update

Open Family Filter TV on Google Play and tap Update, or let Android update it for you. Play rolls a release out gradually, so it can take a little while to appear. If you installed the app directly instead of from Google Play, see the download page; the direct version is updated separately.
