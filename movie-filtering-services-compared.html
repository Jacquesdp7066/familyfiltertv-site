Movie filtering services compared

Five ways to get swearing, and sometimes more, out of the films your family watches: Family Filter TV, VidAngel, ClearPlay, Enjoy Movies Your Way and TVGuardian. They differ most in price, in where they run, in what they filter, and in whether they work in your country. The table sets them side by side as of October 2026, including what the others do that Family Filter TV does not.

What the others do that Family Filter TV does not

It is worth being plain about this, because the right choice depends on it.

  • Violence and nudity. VidAngel and ClearPlay filter violence and sex or nudity alongside language, and Enjoy Movies Your Way covers violence through tags. Family Filter TV mutes spoken profanity and nothing else.
  • More platforms. VidAngel and Enjoy Movies Your Way list apps for Roku, Fire TV and iPhone or iPad. Family Filter TV runs only on Android TV, Google TV, Android TV boxes and Android phones, and not on the built-in apps of Samsung, LG and other non-Android TVs.
  • A free plan. Enjoy Movies Your Way lists one. Family Filter TV has a 14-day free trial, then a paid subscription.
  • Hardware for cable and DVD. TVGuardian works from closed captions on a box, which suits a cable or DVD setup where an app cannot run.

What Family Filter TV does differently

Family Filter TV is an app that mutes spoken profanity while the picture keeps playing, using the subtitles for the title. Nothing is cut, blurred or skipped. It works with supported Stremio playback, and it can follow along while you watch Netflix and other apps on an Android TV or Google TV: you pick the title on your Android phone and the TV mutes the swearing. Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through, it works for English titles for now, and it depends on subtitles being available. It is not limited to one country, and one household setting on the phone (off, mild, family or strict) covers every linked device, with three included and up to six.

Which one fits

  • You are in the United States and want language, nudity and violence all filtered: look at VidAngel, ClearPlay or Enjoy Movies Your Way first.
  • You watch on Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV: look at the services whose sites list those devices.
  • You only want swearing muted, and you watch on an Android TV or Google TV: Family Filter TV is built for that.
  • You watch cable or DVD on a TV with closed captions: look at TVGuardian.
  • You are outside the United States: confirm country support on each service's own site before paying.

Questions

Which movie filtering service is cheapest?

On the list prices in the table above, Family Filter TV at US$14.99 a year is the lowest annual price of the paid subscriptions. Enjoy Movies Your Way also has a free plan and lists a premium plan at $7.99 a month, and TVGuardian is a one-off hardware purchase. Prices change, so check each service's own site.

Which of these filter violence and nudity?

VidAngel and ClearPlay filter violence and sex or nudity as well as language, and Enjoy Movies Your Way covers violence and other content through tags. Family Filter TV mutes spoken profanity only and does not filter violence or nudity.

Do any of these work outside the United States?

VidAngel's support pages and ClearPlay's help documentation both say their services are for use in the United States only. Family Filter TV is not restricted to the United States. The sources we checked did not state a country limit for Enjoy Movies Your Way or TVGuardian, so check their sites.

Which of these run on a TV without a computer?

VidAngel and Enjoy Movies Your Way list apps for TV platforms on their own sites. ClearPlay runs as a Chrome extension on a computer that you connect to the TV. TVGuardian is a separate box that reads closed captions. Family Filter TV runs on Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone.

Is Family Filter TV affiliated with any of these services?

No. Family Filter TV is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to VidAngel, ClearPlay, Enjoy Movies Your Way, TVGuardian or any streaming service.
