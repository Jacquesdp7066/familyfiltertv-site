Choosing a movie filtering service: what to check

Several services and apps exist for taking swearing, and sometimes more, out of the films your family watches. They differ in price, in where they run, in what they filter and in where they are available. This page lists what to check, and gives the answer for Family Filter TV. For any other service, its own site is the place to get current, accurate answers.

Questions worth asking of any service

  • Does it filter only language, or violence and nudity as well?
  • Does it run on the device you watch on, and with the streaming services you use?
  • Is it available in your country?
  • What does it cost over a full year, and is there a free trial or a refund period?
  • Who makes it, and what does it do with your data? Read its privacy policy.

What Family Filter TV does

Family Filter TV is an app that mutes spoken profanity while the picture keeps playing, using the subtitles for the title. It works with supported Stremio playback, and it can follow along while you watch Netflix and other apps on an Android TV or Google TV: you pick the title on your Android phone and the TV mutes the swearing. Muting is best effort, so a line may slip through, it works for English titles for now, and it depends on subtitles being available. One household setting on the phone (off, mild, family or strict) covers every linked device, with three included and up to six.

Other services

Other services exist, including VidAngel, ClearPlay, Enjoy Movies Your Way and TVGuardian. They are made by other companies. We do not describe what they filter, where they run, what they cost or where they are available. Check their own sites for current details.

Questions

What should I check before choosing a movie filtering service?

Check what it filters (language, violence, nudity), which devices and streaming services it works with, which countries it is available in, what it costs per year, whether there is a free trial, and who makes it. Each service states these on its own site, and they change.

What does Family Filter TV filter?

Spoken profanity only. It mutes the words while the picture keeps playing. It does not filter violence or nudity.

Which devices does Family Filter TV run on?

Android TV and Google TV sets and boxes, with a companion app on an Android phone. Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs are muted from the phone over Wi-Fi. It does not run on Roku, Fire TV or Apple TV.

What does Family Filter TV cost?

There is a 14-day free trial, then a 30-day or 1-year plan (current prices are shown in Google Play), with three devices included and up to six. Google Play charges in local currency.

Is Family Filter TV affiliated with any other filtering service?

No. Family Filter TV is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to VidAngel, ClearPlay, Enjoy Movies Your Way, TVGuardian or any streaming service.
